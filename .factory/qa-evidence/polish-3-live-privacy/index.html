Policy · effective August 28, 2026

How your CSV data is handled

CSV Import Cleanroom performs parsing, mapping, validation, and export inside your browser. Spreadsheet rows are never sent to Sociobot or a third party.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your active workspace and saved recipes in browser IndexedDB so work can survive a refresh. It stores a purchase license and a cached verification result in localStorage. Reset workspace removes active work. Delete removes an individual saved recipe. You can also clear this site’s browser data.

Network requests

The installed app checks Sociobot’s billing API only when you provide a Cleanroom Plus license, and then no more than once a day. That request includes the license token but never spreadsheet data. Checkout is hosted by Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record, and their checkout privacy terms apply there.

Analytics and tracking

There are no advertising pixels, third-party analytics, tracking cookies, remote fonts, or runtime CDN scripts in the app.

Offline cache

A service worker caches the app shell and artwork so the cleanroom can open offline. This cache contains application files, not a server-side copy of your CSV.

Questions

For privacy requests, contact privacy@sociobot.in. Because spreadsheet data never reaches us, we cannot retrieve or recover it.
